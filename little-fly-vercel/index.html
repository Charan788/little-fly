What is powering this creature?

This is a handcrafted, eight-node sensory–motor model inspired by simple animal behavior. It does not use downloaded FlyWire neurons or reproduce a fruit fly’s brain. Light and obstacle sensors feed steering nodes, which drive two virtual motors. Node activity changes gradually; disabling nodes changes the actual controller.

Green connections carry light responses. Orange connections carry obstacle responses. Solid lines excite; dashed lines inhibit. The left steering node speeds the right motor and slows the left; the right steering node does the reverse. Physical collision handling keeps the creature out of solid objects, even when its obstacle sensing is disabled.

A real FlyWire version would require an identified circuit, its connectivity data, and additional assumptions about neuron dynamics and sensory and motor mappings. Explore the FlyWire dataset ↗
